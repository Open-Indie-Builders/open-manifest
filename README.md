# OPEN/ Manifest

> **Build in public. Share real work. Stay independent.**

The **OPEN/ Manifest** is the central hub and entry point for the **Open Indie Builder** ecosystem. It serves as a directory and philosophical statement for a suite of interoperable tools designed specifically for solopreneurs, indie hackers, and small teams who prioritize efficiency, data ownership, and subtractive design over enterprise bloat.

## 0. The Scope

The **OPEN/ Manifest** is the philosophical foundation of the ecosystem. It is a minimalist workspace designed to establish the core values of "Anti-marketing" and "Data Sovereignty," providing a clear direction for the development of all subsequent tools. Its primary purpose is to define the boundaries of our community: those who value work made visible over the theater of traditional business.

## 1. The Manifest

The following principles govern the work of the Open Indie Builder:

### Build in public.
- Not when it is ready.
- Not when it looks good.
- **While it is still unclear.**

### Share the work.
- The thinking.
- The numbers.
- Revenue. MRR. Mistakes.

### Do not turn it into content.
- Do not smooth the edges.
- Do not hide the doubts.

---

**This is not marketing. This is not storytelling.**

**It is work, made visible.**

---

**TOOLS ARE OPTIONAL. OPENNESS IS NOT.**

*If this feels natural, you already belong here.*
